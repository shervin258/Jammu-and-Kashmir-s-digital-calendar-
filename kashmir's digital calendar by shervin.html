Beauty That Builds Livelihoods

Jammu & Kashmir is not only a land of breathtaking landscapes but also a home to talented artisans, farmers, guides, weavers and entrepreneurs. Responsible tourism can create employment, support small businesses and help preserve cultural heritage.

Our message: Travel responsibly. Buy authentic local crafts. Respect nature and culture. Support decent work and sustainable economic growth.
